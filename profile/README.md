<p align="center"><strong>Audio tools for night people.</strong></p>

<p align="center">NIGHTZUNE is a small software studio building audio tools for the hours when the city sleeps.</p>

---

### What we make

Software for music production: precise, quiet tools with the soul of hardware. Engines that stay out of the
way, and interfaces that feel like real instruments instead of windows on a screen.

### How we work

- **One hero control.** Every device has a single control the eye finds first. Everything else serves it.
- **Light from within.** Nothing is painted neon. The glow comes from behind glass, through a drilled hole,
  from inside a tube.
- **Depth in the shadows.** Panels are slabs, holes are drilled, keys press into the surface. A flat
  surface is always a mistake.
- **Devices that rest.** Nothing pulses without the music. When the transport stops, the room goes quiet.

### Currently

Products are in development. Releases are announced when they are ready, not before.
